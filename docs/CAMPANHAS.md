# Banners editoriais

Imagens produzidas com a ferramenta nativa de geração de imagens em 28/10/2026, a partir de fotografias demonstrativas já presentes no catálogo. A primeira referência fornecida pelo usuário orientou a fotografia de calçado em uso; a segunda orientou a sobreposição de um produto grande sobre o título. As referências não foram copiadas como arte pronta.

Preserve the EXACT boot design, brown matte leather, cap toe stitching, lace pattern, side logo, black sole and heel, rear pull tab, proportions and right-facing orientation. Do not invent a Chelsea boot. Remove ALL the white background and the original ground plane; genuinely transparent alpha background. Keep the entire boot with comfortable 5% margins. Improve lighting into an elegant studio key light from upper left, rich leather detail and tactile realistic texture, subtle soft contact shadow below only. The product should occupy 90% of the square canvas and remain a faithful product photograph. No text, no border, no extra objects.

She wears a modest dark chocolate skirt, entirely nonsexual fashion editorial styling. Place the legs and sandals entirely in the LEFT 58 percent of the composition. The RIGHT 42 percent must be uncluttered plain softly lit dark espresso brown seamless studio background, reserved for live website text. Overall dark warm coffee and taupe palette, no yellow or orange cast. Sculptural side lighting, realistic skin and soft long floor shadows, sophisticated magazine footwear campaign, cinematic but natural, beautiful leather and gold metal detail. Ground plane and wall form a continuous seamless studio sweep. Entire frame filled with the photography, no layout mockup, NO TEXT, NO LETTERS, NO WATERMARK, NO LOGO overlay.
